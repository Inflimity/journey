Before you ask

Questions, answered.

Do you work with small businesses?

Yes. Most of my clients are small and growing businesses: training firms, consultancies, construction and service companies. Every project gets a fixed price agreed before any work starts.

How long does a website take?

Usually 7 to 10 days from the first call to a live site, for a typical small-business website with online booking.

Can you work with me if I'm in another time zone?

Yes. I work remotely, overlap with US, UK and EU business hours, and reply within one working day.

What kind of custom software do you build?

Tools that replace spreadsheets, forms and manual processes: client portals, dashboards, data pipelines and internal apps, built with Next.js, Node.js, Python and Supabase.

What is included in a security check?

A review of your website or codebase against common attack paths such as exposed keys, injection and broken access control, with a plain-English report and fixes ranked by risk.

How do payments work?

A fixed price agreed up front, with 50% to start and the rest on delivery.
